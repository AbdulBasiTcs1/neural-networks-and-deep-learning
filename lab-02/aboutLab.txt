This is the Lab Manual : Lab02 =>

Objective: 
The objective of this lab is to learn about numpy, a python library, commonly used for 
implementing neural networks.

Activity Outcomes: 
The activities provide hands - on practice with the following topics 
• Implement vectors and matrixes. 
• Perform vectors and matrix operations such as dot product, addition, subtraction, 
reshaping, etc  
• Perform indexing and slicing. 
• Implement random matrixes 
• Implement algebraic operations such as transpose
